# RESEARCH BASIS

## Existing Indian ecosystem

Official MHA/CCTNS and ICJS material already describes crime-data integration and criminal network/link analysis.

Therefore:

> Do not claim that a criminal Knowledge Graph itself is novel.

## Research themes

### Berlusconi et al. (2016)

Research on link prediction in criminal networks showed that network structure can be used to identify potentially missing links.

Important interpretation:
A predicted link is an investigative lead, not proof.

### Calderoni et al. (2020)

Research on robust link prediction in a Sicilian Mafia case compared multiple network/link-prediction methods and demonstrated the importance of network structure and robustness to incomplete/noisy data.

### Ahmadi et al. (2023)

Research investigated inductive and transductive link prediction for criminal-network analysis using case-report data.

### ROXANNE

Provides research-oriented anonymized criminal/case-network data useful for experimentation.
